ClaimProof — Distributor Interview

15 min · research, not a pitch
Company / revenue:Name / role:Date:Product category:
Rules: Ask about the past, not hypotheticals, until Q6. Don't describe the product before Q6. Write down the numbers they actually say ("about 40 a month", "$180k last year"), not your impression. Silence is fine; let them fill it. Target roles: controller/CFO, AR manager, customer service manager.
  1. How many damage or shortage credits did you issue last year, and roughly what total value?
    Probe: Is that tracked, or a guess? Biggest single claim? What share of revenue?
    Tests: unverified credits are a meaningful dollar amount
  2. How do claims arrive, and who approves them?
    Probe: Email, phone, portal, rep? Who issues the credit, and is there a dollar limit before sign-off?
    Tests: someone owns the problem
  3. What evidence do you require? What happens when it's missing?
    Probe: Do you match photos to the shipment (BOL, POD, label)? Do you ever credit with no photo at all?
  4. Have you caught a fake or recycled photo, or suspected one?
    Probe: Tell me about the last time. How did you notice? What did you do with that account?
  5. Are some accounts claiming far more than others? What do you do about it?
    Probe: Do you know their claim rate vs. other accounts? Has it cost you a customer relationship?
    Tests: budget and appetite to act
  6. (Last) If every claim came through a link that verified photos and matched them to the shipment, would your customers use it? Would you require it above a certain dollar amount?
    Probe: What threshold? Which customers would push back? Who would sign off on paying for it?
    Tests: customers will accept a capture link (likely deal-breaker)

Verdict

  • Kill if credits are small, or they'd never ask customers to use a link.
  • Promising if credits are material, approval is loose, and they'd require the link above a threshold.
  • Ask for one intro to a peer distributor.
