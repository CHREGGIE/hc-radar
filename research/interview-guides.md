# Interview guides

Rule for both: you're researching, not pitching. 15 minutes. Ask about the past, not hypotheticals,
until the last question. Write down numbers they actually say.

## New Practice Radar — vendors who sell to new practices
Targets: dental/medical supply reps, medical billing companies, healthcare staffing agencies,
EHR resellers, malpractice insurance agents, healthcare marketing agencies.

1. How do you find out a new practice is opening today?
2. How many new-practice customers did you sign last year? What's one worth to you per year?
3. How early do you usually hear about them? What happens when you hear late?
4. What tools or lists do you pay for now? What do they cost? What's wrong with them?
5. Which specialties and geographies matter most to you?
6. Who on your team would use a weekly list, and what would they do with it?
7. (Last) If you got a weekly list of new practices in your territory with owner name and address,
   what would that be worth per month? Would you try a free month?

**Signal of a real market:** they currently pay for something worse, or spend rep time hunting manually,
and can put a dollar value on an early lead.

## ClaimProof — mid-size distributors
Targets: controller/CFO, AR manager, customer service manager.

1. How many damage or shortage credits did you issue last year, and roughly what total value?
2. How do claims arrive, and who approves them?
3. What evidence do you require? What happens when it's missing?
4. Have you caught a fake or recycled photo, or suspected one?
5. Are some accounts claiming far more than others? What do you do about it?
6. (Last) If every claim came through a link that verified photos and matched them to the shipment,
   would your customers use it? Would you require it above a certain dollar amount?

**Kill criteria:** credits are small, or they'd never ask customers to use a link.

## Outreach note (adapt per person)

> practices]. Could I ask you a few questions for 15 minutes? Not selling anything; happy to share
> what I learn from the other conversations.
